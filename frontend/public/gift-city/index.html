Questions about GIFT City financial research

Can I use an official FX reference as my conversion quote?

A reference can support a dated valuation. An executable conversion requires your provider's actual rate, fees, validity, access and settlement terms. Read the FX conversion guide.

How can I compare the cost of financing gold?

Keep metal value, term funding cost, included fees and FX conversion separate. The GIFT City scenario accepts your price and funding assumptions; the two-scenario comparison shows how fees and day counts affect cost. Read the funding-cost guide.

Does this desk publish a live bullion price or confirm IIBX access?

No. The calculator uses the bullion quote you supply. Current participant eligibility, contracts and delivery requirements must be checked in IIBX documentation and IFSCA's metals and commodities material.

What is different about this research workflow?

Source observations, derived comparisons and your entered assumptions remain distinguishable. The available exports retain the source dates and assumptions so another reviewer can check the basis of the result. Missing evidence remains visible.
