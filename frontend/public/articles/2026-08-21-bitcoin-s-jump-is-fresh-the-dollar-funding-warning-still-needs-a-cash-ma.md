Bitcoin’s Coinbase daily close rose $4,677.21 to $77,689.08, a 3.5-sigma move against its own history. It is the fresh market change in the latest dispatch. It does not, by itself, confirm tighter dollar funding.

Seiche’s board reads 45 out of 100, or STRAIN, because the dated reserve path is doing the work: reserves are below a fitted kink and Treasury settlements lie ahead. Current overnight pricing says something different. SOFR remains below IORB, the Standing Repo Facility is unused, and broad market stress is low. The evidence supports a pre-registered funding test, not a declaration that scarcity has arrived.

## The mechanism or historical setup

The proposed chain begins with reserves. The latest reserve reading was $2,935B, down $8.8B on the week. The ledger attributes a $16.0B absorption to the foreign RRP pool and a $14.3B balance-sheet contraction, partly offset by a $10.9B residual release. Its six legs sum to the reserve change to the dollar, though $8.0B of the balance sheet, or 0.12% of assets, remains in an unnamed residual.

The pressure is dated. A $266B Treasury auction settlement falls on Aug. 27 and a $183B settlement on Aug. 31. Seiche’s reserve path identifies both as pressure windows while reserves are below its estimated kink. The weather component is saturated at 100.0 and contributes 11.0 points to the composite, making the calendar the dominant driver.

That is a transmission hypothesis, not a mechanical rule that settlement cash must produce stress. The question is whether dated cash removal, against lower reserve buffers, raises the price at which dealers and lenders supply overnight capacity.

The bounded historical guide is the September 2025 tax-date squeeze, when a tax-date drain and Treasury settlement pressure briefly widened the gap between secured and unsecured overnight rates. Its value is methodological: specify the date and transmission path, then judge the funding tape. A calendar event is not itself a crisis signal.

## Quantified evidence with as-of context

At the latest reserve observation, the fitted reserve-demand kink was $3,702.8B, against reserves of $2,935.3B, a distance of -$767.5B. The fit’s R-squared is 0.616. This is a structural estimate, not an observed market-clearing threshold.

For Aug. 27, the reserve path estimates $2,942.7B of reserves and a worst-case $2,881.4B around the $266.0B settlement. For Aug. 31, it estimates $2,941.0B and a worst-case $2,870.0B around the $183.0B settlement. These are Seiche scenarios, not observed reserve prints.

Foreign officials cut Fed custody Treasuries by $80.7B over 13 weeks while foreign RRP parking rose $55.6B. The combined official footprint nevertheless fell $25.0B to $2,960B: 31% of the custody decline left the official sector rather than rotating within it.

The divergence is notable but incomplete. Plumbing stands at the 58th percentile of its own history, market pricing at the 29th percentile: plumbing leads price by 30 percentile points. The pooled five-business-day event read is 6.4%, while a separate model read is 2.6%. Neither is a literal probability: the model does not beat climatology on probability levels.

## The strongest counter-case

The counter-case can defeat the thesis because it is based on the market that should react first. At the latest secured-rate observation, SOFR was 3.62%, 0.9bp below IORB; IORB was 3.65% at its latest observation. EFFR was 3.63%. A secured overnight rate below the administered reserve rate is an abundance signal, not a broad scarcity print.

Nor is the official backstop being tested. Standing Repo Facility accepted take-up was $0.00B at its latest observation. Market stress was at the 29th percentile of its history. VIX was 14.89 and the high-yield option-adjusted spread was 2.73%.

The board’s own month-end output is restrained. For Aug. 31, the published forecast is 2.2bp, within a band of -1.8bp to 6.9bp, with severity at 1 on its calm-to-extreme scale. The model estimate is 9.0bp, but the published estimate is the naïve one. If the funding tape does not change, this evidence outweighs a calendar-led strain call.

## A falsifiable next test

Aug. 27 is the first observable test: $266B settles while reserves sit below the estimated kink. Confirmation requires independent cash-market evidence that secured funding is becoming less abundant: SOFR moving toward or above IORB, meaningful Standing Repo Facility use, or a broader increase in the market-stress reading.

The thesis fails this test if SOFR remains below IORB, facility take-up remains negligible, and risk measures stay contained through the settlement window. The Aug. 31 $183B settlement supplies a second test. The point of dated tests is to make the claim gradeable rather than rhetorical.

Bitcoin’s move is not that confirmation. It is a fresh market signal; the funding argument stands or falls on overnight cash plumbing.

## Follow the pressure chain

The working sequence is reserve reduction; a below-kink estimate; Treasury settlement and calendar demands; potentially less dealer and lender balance-sheet elasticity; then a secured-rate response. The earlier links are observed inputs or Seiche derivations. The later links are the causal proposition under test.
