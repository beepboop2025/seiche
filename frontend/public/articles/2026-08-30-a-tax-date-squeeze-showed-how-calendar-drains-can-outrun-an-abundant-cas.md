## The mechanism or historical setup

The September 2025 squeeze combined a tax-date drain with Treasury settlement pressure. The desk guide describes the sequence as dated cash removal, limited dealer capacity, then a visible funding response. SOFR rose 18bp over EFFR. The episode is dated 2025-09-15; the analog ends on 2025-09-12. The engine records an event within five business days and a maximum five-business-day move of 9.0bp.

The lesson is not that a calendar date creates a crisis. A dated drain becomes consequential only if the cash and balance-sheet capacity needed to absorb it are unavailable at the relevant point in the system. The historical episode therefore supplies a disciplined sequence to observe: identify the scheduled drain, watch whether secured funding shifts relative to its policy anchor, and then examine whether the standing backstop is used.

The desk guide calls the episode smaller than September 2019, but treats it as a cleaner modern test of the same mechanism. That is the appropriate use of the replay: a calendar event is a pre-registered test, not an alarm.

## The strongest counter-case

The counter-case can defeat the thesis: current funding prices do not corroborate scarcity. SOFR is below IORB.
