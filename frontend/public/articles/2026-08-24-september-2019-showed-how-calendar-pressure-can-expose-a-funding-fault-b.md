The question is how dollar-funding capacity can tighten before broader markets recognize it. ## The mechanism or historical setup

The September 2019 repo spike followed the convergence of corporate tax payments and Treasury settlement needs with a reserve system that had less immediately deployable cash than calm headline measures suggested. The break appeared first in secured overnight funding: SOFR reached 5.25% and GC reached 10% on September 17, even though the broader risk-asset story had not announced a crisis.

The lesson is that aggregate reserves and usable intermediation capacity are different objects. Calendar flows move cash through the system. When the institutions facing outflows cannot readily deploy cash, or dealers and lenders cannot expand balance sheets enough to intermediate those flows, the marginal cash needed to clear secured funding can become scarce locally.

The historical-comparison engine assigns this episode a similarity of 0.581. That is a construction-point-in-time comparison, not a publication-vintage backtest or forecast. Similarity does not imply recurrence.

A settlement date is not itself a funding shock. Reserves may redistribute, private balance sheets may accommodate the flows, and the backstop may remain unused because scarcity is absent. The pooled five-business-day event read is 5.8%. The model-based measure is 1.7% and does not beat climatology on probability levels. The desk’s book remains neutral.

September 2019 establishes a mechanism worth monitoring. It does not establish that the mechanism is binding now.

## A falsifiable next test

The observable test is the August 27 settlement and the August 31 month-end settlement. The pressure thesis gains support only if those calendar drains coincide with deterioration in observed overnight plumbing: SOFR no longer trading below IORB, or meaningful Standing Repo Facility use.

The thesis fails this test if the settlements pass while SOFR remains below IORB, the facility remains unused in size, and secured funding conditions do not deteriorate. A calendar model must earn its warning through market confirmation; it cannot recast a calm outcome as delayed confirmation.

For August 31, the published naive forecast is 2.2bp, with a band from -1.8bp to 6.9bp and severity of 1 on the desk’s calm-to-extreme scale. The desk publishes the naive forecast rather than the model forecast, preserving the distinction between a structural flag and a confirmed funding event.

## Follow the pressure chain

Follow the sequence: reserve drainage and official-sector reallocation; dated Treasury settlements; the distribution of deployable cash and balance sheet; secured overnight pricing and backstop use; then any broader market response.

That ordering is the historical lesson. In September 2019, the break appeared first in the price of secured overnight balance sheet. A calm risk tape did not disprove a funding problem. Equally, today’s calm overnight tape is affirmative evidence against declaring that problem already present.
