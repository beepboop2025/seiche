Research workflow / capital markets

A price move matters when it finds a balance sheet.

Capital-market transmission research asks how Treasury supply, duration, credit, volatility, inventories or crowded positions could become cash demand. It does not call every repricing a funding shock, and it does not mistake a registered source family for a complete capital-market dataset.

How to test capital-market transmission

Build a chain whose links can fail independently.

Start with an exact observed market fact. Identify the yield, spread, volatility reference, auction, inventory or positioning report with its native unit and date. Then state the proposed financing bridge—settlement cash, repo, collateral, margin, dealer warehouse or commodity working capital—and test it against eligible funding observations.

Route the last mile. LiquiLens can test institution-level balance-sheet exposure; Undertow can test crowding, provider fragility and exit context. Their outputs remain separate evidence, not confirmation that Seiche's mechanism is causal.

Citation choices

Match the URL to the claim

A raw value, a derived transform and a fleet synthesis need different citations.

observed Raw market fact

Cite the exact series or original publisher, unit, observation date and release clock.

derived Seiche interpretation

Cite the capital-market page or API revision, generation time, evidence status and input clocks.

structural Registered source family

Describe it as coverage metadata unless the response also supplies an eligible observation.

unavailable Unsupported bridge

State which link failed. Do not fill it with a nearby series, zero, or a causal assertion.

Frequently asked questions

Transmission without overclaiming

These visible answers match the structured FAQ.

What does capital-market transmission mean here?

Capital-market transmission is the testable path from a public market repricing through positions and intermediary balance sheets into settlement, collateral, margin or other cash demands. Seiche treats that path as a hypothesis until eligible funding evidence confirms it.

Does Seiche cover every stock, bond and derivative?

No. Seiche publishes a bounded macro transmission layer over registered Treasury, credit, volatility, dealer, positioning and commodity source families. It is not a global security master, consolidated tape, fundamentals database, order book or execution venue.

Does co-movement prove that a capital-market move caused funding stress?

No. Timing and co-movement can identify a mechanism worth testing, but they do not prove causality. A responsible brief states the bridge, checks funding confirmation, publishes counterevidence and leaves unsupported links unresolved.
