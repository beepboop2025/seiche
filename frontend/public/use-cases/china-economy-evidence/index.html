Direct answer

How to research China economic evidence

Ask four questions before interpreting a number: what is the official series, what period does it measure, when was it released or captured, and may the value be republished?

Use Palimpsest for revision-safe public observations. Its China Observatory retains reference, release, collection and revision clocks, evidence coverage and explicit abstention, plus separate deletion, blocking and record-integrity evidence.

Use Seiche for macro identity, rights and transmission context. The public selector binds release-reviewed NBS series identities to exact labels, semantics and source references. Its structural response is unsigned; a restricted response can carry verified Seiche owner-attested capture provenance, but neither response publishes values or becomes an NBS signature. Keep these outputs separate in this workflow; do not turn censor attention into an economic measurement.

Frequently asked questions

China evidence, plainly

These visible answers match the structured FAQ.

Does Seiche publish China macro observations?

No. Seiche's public China macro contract publishes release-reviewed series identities, labels, semantics, source references and provenance states. It does not publish the restricted values, histories or raw NBS exports.

What does Palimpsest add to China economy research?

Palimpsest supplies revision-safe public economic observations and a separate record of information controls, deletion, network interference and record integrity. Those outputs keep their own clocks and remain outside this workflow's Seiche world-markets projection and funding composite. Seiche's separate Far Basin model-entry gate remains governed by its own minimum-observation policy.

Is Seiche capture time the date of the economic observation?

No. Knowledge time records when Seiche captured or learned a revision. It is not the period measured by the statistic, the original publisher's release time or proof that a restricted value may be redistributed.
