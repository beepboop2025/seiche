What the China contract proves

The unsigned structural catalog proves only what Seiche's reviewed code recognizes. A verified revision additionally proves continuity of Seiche's owner-attested evidence chain: signed manifest identity, public projection, trusted signer, capture clock and predecessor relationship. The public receipt does not expose raw-data hashes or sizes. Neither state proves NBS signed an export or grants downstream redistribution rights.

The public object is a source catalog or provenance receipt, never a China data feed. It tells a researcher or agent exactly which four NBS tables and rows Seiche recognizes, how each source field should be interpreted, and whether a verified Seiche owner-attested capture exists.

knowledge_time is the evidence-capture clock. It is deliberately excluded from World Markets observation clocks, freshness, coverage scores, CN-CNY gauges and every forecasting or ranking path.

Frequently asked questions

Do not overread the receipt

These visible answers exactly match the structured FAQ above.

Does Seiche publish China NBS economic values?

No. The public China macro contract always publishes four release-reviewed series identities and semantics. Only a restricted response adds verified Seiche owner-attested capture provenance. Raw exports, monthly observations and histories remain restricted pending redistribution review.

Is the owner attestation an NBS digital signature?

No. The Ed25519 attestation proves that a trusted Seiche operator captured and signed a specific export envelope. It does not claim that the National Bureau of Statistics signed the file.

What does knowledge_time mean for China macro evidence?

knowledge_time records when the owner-attested evidence was captured and knowable to Seiche. It is not the economic observation month and never advances the world-market observation clock.

Not investment advice

This is evidence provenance and source semantics, not a forecast, a China growth score or a recommendation to trade.

Citation protocol

Cite identity and evidence state

If the API says unavailable, cite only the structural catalog. If it says restricted, add the returned revision ID, owner-attestation signer and knowledge time—never invent an observation value or date.
