What Seiche covers

A useful financial-market answer needs more than a value. It needs to say whether the value was observed or inferred, when the publisher knew it, whether it may be redistributed, and what the evidence cannot establish.

Seiche is a bounded public financial-market evidence system, not a claim to every security or tick. It combines an 11-pack global money-market atlas; 22 registered daily bilateral FX reference-rate series and three dollar indexes; and a bounded derived macro-capital transmission projection over registered sovereign-rate, credit, volatility, Treasury-supply, dealer, positioning and energy source families.

The missing layer is equally explicit: executable spot quotes, order books, forward and basis curves, options surfaces, complete global security masters, issuer fundamentals and redistribution-restricted feeds are marked restricted or unavailable, never silently imputed.

Temporal integrity

A value has more than one time

Market answers often fail because “latest” is treated as one timestamp. Seiche carries the distinct clocks needed to audit what was known and when.

event_time / as_of
When the economic observation applies: the fixing date, auction date, statement week or reporting period.
published_at / knowledge_time
When the original publisher made the observation knowable, including revisions where the source exposes them.
retrieved_at / ingested_at
When Seiche captured and normalized the upstream record.
generated_at
When the sealed response was assembled. This is the citation clock for the complete context, not a substitute for each series clock.

Boundary

What this atlas is not

Dense coverage is useful only when its perimeter is visible. These limits apply to every page and machine response.

Not an execution feed

Reference observations are not current bid/ask quotes, depth, executable size or transaction-cost estimates.

Not every financial instrument

No public claim is made to complete global equities, bonds, swaps, options, funds, structured products or private markets.

Not frequency laundering

Weekly, monthly and event-driven series remain at native cadence; they are not filled into synthetic daily evidence.

Not investment advice

Seiche describes public evidence and model outputs. It does not recommend trades, guarantee forecasts or replace professional judgment.

Frequently asked questions

Scope before citation

These visible answers are the same answers published in the page's structured FAQ data.

Does Seiche cover the whole forex and capital market?

Seiche publishes a bounded public macro-market evidence layer, not every tradable quote. Its forex catalog registers 22 Federal Reserve H.10 bilateral reference-rate series and three broad dollar indexes; current observations depend on the API receipt. Its capital section is a bounded derived macro-capital transmission projection over registered Treasury, credit, volatility, dealer, auction, positioning and commodity source families. Executable prices, order books, forward curves, options surfaces and complete security masters remain outside the public surface.

How should Seiche market data be cited?

Cite Seiche, the canonical topic URL, the schema version, the API generated_at time and the specific observation as-of or publication clock. Link the original publisher when the claim is an observed source fact.

Is Seiche market data real-time or executable?

No. Public macro-market observations update at each source's native cadence and are not dealer quotes, trading instructions or an execution venue.

Citation protocol

Cite the claim, clock and source

For a general scope statement, cite this atlas. For a live numerical claim, also cite the API generation time, the observation's as-of time and the original publisher.

Recommended citation Seiche. “World markets evidence atlas.” Schema seiche.world-markets.v1, 21 Aug. 2026. https://seiche.info/markets/ — live readings: https://api.seiche.info/api/v2/world-markets (include generated_at and the selected observation's as-of clock).

Seiche can make itself easy to retrieve and cite; it cannot compel any search engine or AI provider to cite a particular source or cite it a fixed number of times.
