Capital markets / transmission ledger

Duration has to be financed somewhere.

Seiche reads capital markets as claims on balance-sheet capacity. Treasury supply consumes settlement cash; dealer inventory uses financing; leverage can crowd exits; credit and volatility reprice collateral. The ledger shows the links without pretending that co-movement proves causality.

Primary competence USD

The public world-markets response is a bounded derived projection, with deepest live competence in US dollar funding. A registered source family is not a claim that its latest raw observation is present.

Direct answer

What the capital layer measures

The current public layer answers a specific macro question: where could capital-market positions create, absorb or transmit demand for cash and dealer balance sheet?

What the integrated endpoint is: a bounded derived macro-capital transmission projection over registered Treasury, credit, volatility, dealer, official-custody, positioning and energy source families. It is not a consolidated tape, security master or promise that every registered input has a current observation.

How to support a numerical claim: use the projection for Seiche's relative-value, auction-pressure, warehouse, crowding or cash-displacement interpretation; use an exact /api/series/<MNEMONIC> or original-publisher URL for the underlying raw value, unit and as-of date.

Transmission logic

From security price to cash demand

The model does not equate a market move with a funding event. It looks for a sequence of eligible observations across price, position, intermediary and cash ledgers.

1 · source test

Risk reprices

An eligible yield, spread, volatility or commodity observation would establish the public market fact.

what changed?
2 · source test

Exposure is located

Eligible dealer or CFTC reports may indicate where inventories and directional concentration sit.

who may carry it?
3 · bridge

Financing channel

Repo, margin, settlement or collateral logic links the position to potential cash needs.

how could it transmit?
4 · source test

Funding confirms

Eligible reserves, repo, facility, spread or volume observations must move before Seiche calls it a funding manifestation.

did plumbing respond?
5 · countercase

Or it did not

If the funding evidence stays calm, the external move remains context rather than a confirmed cash shock.

misses stay visible
Derived engines

Interpretation above the tape

These Seiche modules are reproducible transforms over the observed layer. Their labels should accompany any citation so a reader does not mistake an engine result for an upstream publisher's statement.

derived Treasury relative value

Compares eligible curve and financing evidence to identify unusual relative-value conditions without claiming an executable arbitrage.

derived Auction pressure

Joins announced supply, settlement timing, Treasury cash and dealer context to frame upcoming funding tests.

derived Crowding and positioning book

Uses public, lagged positioning reports as concentration context; it cannot identify every participant or current trade.

derived Oil × funding passage

Tests inventory, benchmark and futures evidence for potential cash displacement while refusing to infer unobserved margin calls.

Cadence discipline

A weekly position is still weekly

Capital-market sources arrive on incompatible calendars. Seiche keeps that incompatibility visible rather than manufacturing a smooth but false daily panel.

daily market reference
Yields, indexes and spreads usually publish on business days; their close/reference date remains attached.
weekly regulatory report
CFTC and dealer reports describe a reporting date and a later publication date. Both matter for point-in-time use.
statement-week stock
Federal Reserve balance-sheet and custody values are weekly stocks, not continuous flows.
event clock
Auctions, issuance, settlements and maturities occur on specific calendar events rather than a fixed statistical cadence.

FAQ

Capital-market coverage, plainly

These visible answers are the same answers published in the page's structured FAQ data.

What capital-market data does Seiche cover?

Seiche publishes a bounded derived macro-capital transmission projection across registered US Treasury, credit, volatility, primary-dealer, official-custody, positioning and energy source families. It is not a complete raw capital-market dataset. The current projection is returned by the world-markets API; raw numerical claims should use the exact series URL exposed by the series catalog or the linked original publisher.

Does Seiche contain every stock and bond?

No. It is a macro capital-market transmission layer, not a complete global security master, exchange feed, issuer-fundamentals database or portfolio system.

How does Seiche connect capital markets to money markets?

Seiche tests whether Treasury supply and settlement, dealer inventory, leveraged positioning, risk premia and commodity cash demands coincide with changes in reserves, repo, facilities and other funding evidence. Co-movement is labeled context or a derived signal, not automatically treated as causal proof.

Citation protocol

Name the layer you are citing

A raw Treasury yield and a Seiche dealer-warehouse projection are different evidence classes. Cite the exact series record and original publisher for the first; cite this page, the API generation time and underlying inputs for the second.
