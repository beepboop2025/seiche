# Pilot acceptance and commercial validation

This release makes collection and contribution operational. It does not itself
create contributor permissions, signed partnerships, users, revenue or world coverage.

The first customer hypothesis is an agent operator, platform or research buyer
that needs attributable activity evidence for due diligence and operational review.
Public discovery supplies leads and context; consented outcome feeds supply depth.
The differentiator to test is traceable identity → submitted action → independently
observed outcome, with missing evidence exposed.

## Four-week pilot

1. Review three prospective independent operators/platforms and their rights before
   enrollment. Select one economic domain and named source population for each cohort.
2. Enroll a small account panel and verify identity/time/version links. Begin collecting
   new events only. Public metadata counts are not private participants.
3. For each participant, reconcile an agreed recent interval against the source's own
   execution/receipt denominator. Report ingestion coverage, reconciliation coverage,
   missing outcomes, source lag, duplicate conflicts and delivery failures separately.
4. Produce private evidence dossiers and ask buyers to use them for an actual decision.
   Record independent feedback and paid pilot commitments if obtained. No synthetic
   traffic or test events count toward those outcomes.

Suggested pilot engineering targets, to agree with each operator: at least 95% of
authorized source events ingested in the chosen window, at least 90% of eligible
fills independently reconciled, and all discrepancies explicitly categorized.
These are proposed targets, not achieved production metrics. Global denominators
remain unknown. Do not evaluate profit using only the reconciled subset.

## Buyer questions ready for interviews

- Which decision requires evidence today, and what records currently support it?
- What would make an agent/account attribution credible enough to use?
- Which missing outcomes or false labels would invalidate a report?
- What source rights and retention constraints apply to the intended use?
- Would a private evidence report or an aggregate cohort dataset fit the workflow?
- What would justify a paid pilot, and who can authorize it?

Outreach has not been sent. The public pilot form records interest only. Validate
the buyer, source access and willingness to pay before treating this as demand.

## Expansion gates

Add a broker/merchant integration only after a named source authorizes it and its
receipt/signature semantics can be tested. Add independent merchant settlement and
physical delivery reconciliation before describing those records as verified outcomes.
Add portfolio accounting before publishing returns. Add dedicated RPC/indexing capacity
before broad historical chain coverage. Load-test measured workloads before upgrading
storage: SQLite is the bounded pilot ledger, not a claim of global-scale ingestion.

Institutional dataset release remains conditional on the implemented cohort and
rights gates. Twenty registered names do not satisfy twenty reviewed trading accounts.
Three accounts under one company do not satisfy three independent contributors.
Separate commercial grants do not authorize shared training or record-level resale.

Suggested order: prove attributable activity in one defined cohort; prove repeat
buyer usefulness; expand to several independent platforms; then improve coverage of
those explicitly measured populations. Claiming “most economically active AI agents”
requires a defensible denominator that this release does not establish.
