# Seiche in a connected research review

Reviewed 2026-09-23. Product focus, not a new signal or release claim.

Seiche owns system-funding and connected market context. Use its existing source
health, history, publication clocks and bounded MCP tools to answer a reviewer's
funding question. LiquiLens owns institution evidence; Undertow owns venue-depth
and position-size exit context; Lab owns shared temporal/evaluation contracts.

Start at [the funding research example](https://liquilens.in/start/?task=funding).
The browser review brief lets a reader retain separately dated responses and
export them for discussion. Its deployment identity belongs to the public-site
repository. A snapshot from Seiche cannot update the source date of an
institution filing or an order book.

Prioritize an analyst's recurring funding brief and API integration before
adding another generic AI assistant or forecast family. Measure whether the
reader can reproduce the facts, identify stale/absent inputs and use the output
again. Returned requests and internal monitors are not verified customers.

Startup funding observations help choose interview questions. They do not
validate Seiche's forecasts, establish complete India market coverage, or
authorize new signal weights. No CRR/SLR/LCR/NSFR compliance status for an
individual institution is inferred from a system-funding reading.

The [canonical portfolio plan](https://github.com/beepboop2025/LiquiLens/blob/main/docs/product/PORTFOLIO_FOCUS.md)
holds the buyer experiment, expansion criteria and adjacent-product boundaries.
