# Public legacy-source projections

This route-specific engineering policy preserves acquired snapshots and their source clocks. It does not establish a blanket license for the fleet or historical point-in-time availability.

The completed USD money desk is projected at the MCP, REST and world-markets read boundaries. Known FRED cards and dependent comparisons, diagnostics, headline statistics and narratives return restriction metadata. Their exact metric ID, original source label, observation date, unit and cadence remain. Known NYFed distributions, OFR segment/MMF observations and Treasury TGA/NYFed SRF cards retain their values and clocks. Chart columns are explicitly allowlisted; FRED-dependent columns become null. Unknown metrics and unexpected desk shapes fail closed.

The nine required funding rows remain present. Five are restricted and four independently sourced rows remain numeric. The existing production capture exporter was qualified against the projection: it emits five restricted blank CSV values, retains the four direct values and preserves all nine original dates, units and source labels. An operator may use those dates to reacquire original-publisher data, with explicit omitted comparisons and readiness false; metadata is never substituted for a numeric crosscheck.

The saved world FX/materials projection is metadata-only. The reportcard keeps Treasury auction demand statistics but withholds its FRED-dependent funding narrative. H10/FRED workbench values, crosses and history are withheld; each original source identity and its dated coverage metadata remains. The separate ECB provider is unchanged.

Tests cover actual engine output, immutable cached snapshots, actual MCP dispatch, REST/world boundaries, direct-source retention, unexpected schema/metric IDs, and date/unit/source preservation. Other native derived tools require their own reviewed projections; this document does not claim those gates are complete.
