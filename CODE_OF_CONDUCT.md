# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Seiche respectful, safe, and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity,
sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We act and interact in ways that support an open, evidence-led, and healthy
community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- showing empathy and respect for different viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and accepting specific, constructive feedback;
- focusing criticism on evidence, methods, and code rather than people;
- acknowledging mistakes, correcting the record, and learning from them;
- respecting privacy, source restrictions, and coordinated disclosure; and
- putting the health of the project and community ahead of individual status.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or imagery, unwanted attention, or advances;
- trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- threats, intimidation, stalking, or encouraging others to harass someone;
- manipulating evidence, concealing conflicts of interest, or pressuring others
  to weaken safety or provenance controls; and
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project-operated communication channels, and public spaces when someone is
officially representing Seiche. It also applies to private interactions that
have a clear and material effect on participation in the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `desk@seiche.info` with the subject
`[Seiche Code of Conduct]`. Include links, dates, context, and any outcome needed
for immediate safety. Do not use a public issue for a confidential report.

The repository maintainer is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this
policy. Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, with confidentiality
preserved as far as practical. The maintainer may remove content, issue a
warning, limit participation, temporarily suspend access, or permanently ban a
participant, depending on the impact and pattern of behavior. Retaliation
against a reporter or witness is itself a violation.

If a report concerns the repository owner or cannot safely be handled through
the project, use [GitHub's abuse-reporting channel](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).

## Enforcement guidelines

Consequences are based on context, severity, intent, and repeated behavior:

1. **Correction** — a private explanation and request to correct an isolated,
   low-impact lapse.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning with clear boundaries for continued
   participation.
3. **Temporary restriction** — time-limited loss of interaction or
   representation privileges for sustained or serious misconduct.
4. **Permanent restriction** — permanent removal for harassment, threats,
   deliberate harm, repeated violations, or refusal to respect prior boundaries.
